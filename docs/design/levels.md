# Levels

## Rules

- The arena is horizontal, with the **paddle on the left edge** moving up and down. The back wall is on the right.
- The layout is a grid of **64 columns × 16 rows**. Each cell is twice as tall as it is wide.
- Each brick is **1 column wide × 2 rows tall**, so a full column holds 8 bricks.
- A **square** is a block of 4 columns × 2 rows, which is 4 bricks side by side. Because cells are twice as tall as wide, a square really is square on screen.
- Bricks have **HP**, the number of hits needed to destroy them. The default is 1.
- Every brick is a standard brick unless the level says otherwise.
- **Editable in Godot:** each level is its own scene, and you place bricks and set their HP in the editor (for example, by painting a TileMapLayer).
- Clearing every brick unlocks the next level. Phases and end conditions are described in [overview.md](overview.md#level-phases).

```
 paddle                     brick grid (64 × 16)                       back wall
   │  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
   ▼  │                                                                │ ▼
  ║   │                          o  ball                               │
  ║   │                                                                │
      └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   HUD:  ⏱ 20.0   ● 0
```

Legend: `█` = 1 HP brick, `▓` = high HP brick, `.` = empty, `|` = paddle.

## Level 3: solid wall with a tunnel

- Columns 0–3 (paddle side) and 60–63 (back wall) are empty.
- Columns 4–59 are solid bricks.
- A tunnel 1 brick tall (rows 8–9) runs straight through the wall.
- Column 4, the face of the wall toward the paddle, is high HP.
- The brick rows on both sides of the tunnel (rows 6–7 and 10–11) are high HP.
- The tunnel mouth is left open.
