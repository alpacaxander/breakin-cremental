# Animations

One row per animation, matched to the full asset list. All are horizontal strips `assets/pixel/png/anim_<name>.png`, native 1x pixels, held frames repeated. Rules in `docs/art-style.md` (Animation section). Generated by `assets/pixel/gen.mjs`. fps: 12 for hits and effects, 8 for idle loops.

Hue sets (docs/palette.md Groups): mag (basic bricks), ora (bomb), blue (crit), mint (refresh), red (enemy bricks), sky (paddle), vio (relics: the shield floor), yellow (damage and effects). The arena picks `<hue>_hit`, `<hue>_break` and `<hue>_spawn` by `Tuning.hue(kind)`.

Sections headed Legacy belong to the cut incremental build: still generated, kept as relic and brick candidates, unused unless a row or `docs/content.md` "Reused strips" says otherwise.
