BreakIn cremental UI Preview

Proposed layout: one pachinko cabinet that every screen lives inside, split into three columns. Mock screens are rendered from the real sprite generator at 640×360, shown at 2× like the game. Text is the 3×5 font, HUD size is 2×.

Main menu

The cabinet is the chassis of every screen: a riveted score column on the left, the peg-board field in the middle, a pieces column on the right, rails between. The menu shows it idle: records on the left, the lamp lettering logo in the field, the button column on the right where every screen's actions live. The hovered button is the hot state; the cursor is a chrome arrow with a lit tip.

Shop: upgrade web

The web fills the field. Nodes are yellow lamps with an icon in the glass and rank pips along the bottom row: dim when revealed, lit when owned, hot when maxed. Links light up between two owned nodes. The left column lists cash and owned ranks, with Reset and Menu at the bottom. Hovering a node writes its name, effect, rank and cost into the right column, with Buy under it. Level select and Play sit in the same column, so the player always looks to the same place to act.

Round: placement

Left column is the HUD: cash, combo, timer idle, best combo. The board sits at the top of the field, the placement line is the dashed mint row at 280. The right column is the tray: real piece sprites in slots with a count badge, the selected slot outlined, capacity as pips, controls, Start as one big mint button with its key under it.

Round: play

The tray locks and dims, the paddle owns the field's bottom. The HUD counts: this round's earnings under cash, combo big in payout yellow with its bar under it, timer going danger-red near zero. Nothing in the field but the game.

Round: result

The arena dims under a panel built from the same nine-patch as the columns. The payout is the headline at 4x, then the best combo and what unlocked. Bulbs chase along the top of the field while the readout shows.

Pause

Esc dims the arena and drops the menu column into a small panel. Nothing else changes, so resuming is instant.

Settings

A toggle and a slider in the same lamp language: a mint lamp switch with a chrome knob, a mint tube track with a chrome knob.

Logo and name variants

Treatments of the same lamp lettering at 3×. The first five keep the name, the next three try other spellings, the last six set BREAKIN on one line with BREAK in one treatment and IN in another, then CREMENTAL under it in IN's treatment.

UI sprites

Everything the screens above are built from, at native size. Buttons and the panel are nine-patches: 3px corners stay fixed, edges and glass repeat.
