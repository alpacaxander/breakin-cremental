Neon Cyberpunk Palette

The same 256-slot Sonic Robo Blast 2 base, re-cut for neon cyberpunk. Thirteen hue ramps of ten steps each, lightest tint to deepest shade, and nothing else. The flesh, tan, leaf, salmon, sky and slate ramps go; gold absorbs amber, yellow absorbs cream, green absorbs lime, violet stands alone. Cyan, hot pink and gunmetal come in. Every rule from the old palette stays: near-black base, one hue per meaning, full-saturation accents, no black outlines.

A fight in the new hues
